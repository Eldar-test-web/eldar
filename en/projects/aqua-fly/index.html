Overview

Aqua Fly is a smart rescue-drone concept for water environments: solar-assisted, tracking people on the beach, signalling rescuers on drowning threat, flying to the scene and releasing a rescue buoy. Custom hexagonal airframe engineered for stability over water; CAD and simulation iterate flight dynamics and sensor behaviour.

02

Context

Concept project, design + engineering development

03

Role

Team member - concept, CAD, development

04

Mission

Drowning kills ~236,000 people yearly (project site figure). Aqua Fly answers with fast eyes over water: detect, signal, reach, drop buoyancy.

05

Design

Custom hexagonal airframe for stability; lightweight yet durable for rapid deployment and precise manoeuvre during rescue ops - as shown on the project site.

06

Development

Advanced CAD and simulation tools refine flight dynamics, sensor accuracy and autonomous rescue behaviour for beach environments.

07

Result

Public concept site with mission, design, development and team sections - rapid surveillance and rescue-buoy response over water.
